Wheel speed sensor integration

20% less sensor dropout, IP-67 sealed

The unsprung-side parts that hold the wheel speed PCB: a waterproof enclosure and a magnet-carrying bearing preload nut.

  • Designed the enclosure with integrated O-ring grooves and verified IP-67 sealing with submersion and high-pressure spray.
